# Brainstorming

- An angle is a *lens*, not a topic: who feels the pain, what changes for them,
  what tension the piece resolves. "Cost of idle compute" and "developer time
  saved" are two angles on one launch.
- Give 3-5 that genuinely differ. If they collapse into one idea, you have one
  angle — say so and go deeper instead.
- Each angle: a short label, then one sentence naming the hook and the reader it
  speaks to.
- Recommend one and say why in a sentence. Do not make the user do the ranking.
- Only draft an outline once the user picks a direction.
- If the product is exploratory, propose angles freely but tag the assumptions
  each one leans on.
