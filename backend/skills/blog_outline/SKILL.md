# Blog outline

- Produce an outline, not a finished post: a working title, a one-paragraph
  lede that states the payoff, 3-6 section headings each with 1-2 bullets of
  what it covers, and a closing beat with one next step.
- Lead with the reader's problem or a concrete moment, not the product name.
- Each section heading should read on its own — a skimmer gets the arc from the
  headings alone.
- Introduce the product / idea where it naturally resolves the thing the
  previous section set up.
- Pull only from the scratchpad: its `body`, `sources`, and `angles`. Do not
  invent facts, numbers, or examples. If the scratchpad flags something as an
  open question or an assumption, keep it that way in the outline.
- Markdown: `#` title, then the lede, then `##` section headings with `-`
  bullets.
