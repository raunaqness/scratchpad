# Blog writing

- Lead with the reader's problem or a concrete moment, not the product name.
- State the payoff in the lede so a skimmer knows why to keep reading.
- 3-6 sections, each with a subhead that could be read on its own.
- Show before you tell: an example, a snippet, a walkthrough, then the takeaway.
- Introduce the product where it naturally solves the thing you just described.
- Close with one clear next step.
- Longer than a LinkedIn article is fine; padding is not. Cut any paragraph that
  does not add a fact or move the argument.
