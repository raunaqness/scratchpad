# Marketing campaign

- Produce a compact plan, not a deck. Cover, in this order:
  - **Objective** — one sentence on what the campaign should achieve.
  - **Audience** — who it is for, and the problem they feel.
  - **Core message** — the single idea every asset ladders up to.
  - **Channels** — 2-4 channels and why each fits this audience.
  - **Sequence** — a rough 3-5 step arc (tease → launch → follow-up), one line each.
  - **Assets** — the concrete pieces to make (e.g. "launch post", "blog outline",
    "email 1"), tied to steps.
- Pull only from the scratchpad: its `body`, `sources`, `angles`, `outline`.
  Do not invent budgets, metrics, timelines, partner names, or results. Where the
  scratchpad is thin, name the gap as an open question rather than filling it.
- Keep proposed positioning that the scratchpad marked `[assumption]` labelled as
  an assumption.
- Markdown with `##` headings for each section.
